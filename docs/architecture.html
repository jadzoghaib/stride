Money flow — Stripe Connect
  1. Athlete onboards once via Stripe Express — Stripe collects identity/KYC and bank details directly; Stride stores only the connected account id.
  2. Sponsor accepts a deal → Stride creates a PaymentIntent (destination charge) for the deal amount; the sponsor pays on a Stripe-hosted element — card data never touches Stride servers.
  3. Funds hold until the deliverable is confirmed (deal state accepted → completed), using delayed transfers — the marketplace-escrow pattern.
  4. Stride takes its platform fee as the application fee on the charge (the monetization model, priced per deal).
  5. Stripe webhooks (payment_intent.succeeded, transfer.paid, account.updated) land on a signature-verified endpoint and drive deal-state transitions — processed by the async tier, recorded in the audit log.
  6. Payout to the athlete’s bank on completion; refunds and disputes flow back through the same objects with full lineage.

Why Connect: it removes PCI scope, KYC, payout rails, and dispute machinery from the roadmap entirely. The deals table already models the state machine the webhooks will drive.
